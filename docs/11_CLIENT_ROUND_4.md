## R4-2 New features

| ID | Request | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| R4-2.1 | "Car of the Day: can be selected or voted for" | Staff selection exists (ADM-60/61). New **voting** behind flag `cotd_voting`: from 18:00 Home shows "Wähle das Car of the Day von morgen" with 3 candidates (the Picker's top 3 eligible cars for the next day, never a sample, never the voter's own car); every member account older than 3 days with a verified address has one vote per day, changeable until 17:00; at 17:55 the Picker takes the candidate with the most votes (tie → the Picker's order); a staff choice still wins. **Vote counts are never shown to members** (only "Du hast abgestimmt" and the winner); staff see counts in the admin. Owners of candidates get no push (no guilt/vanity notifications). Abuse: one vote per account and day, rate limit, votes of removed/blocked accounts dropped |
| R4-2.2 | "Likes and comments on posts so users can interact" | Likes exist as private reactions (ADR-413: the post's owner sees who reacted, nobody sees counts — brief rule "no public like counts", kept). Comments exist behind `build_comments` (flat + one reply level, moderated): **switched on in local and staging**, production after the client's OK. The reaction control gets a clear heart "Gefällt mir" as its first type in the UI copy. Public counts would need the client's explicit decision against the brief — asked in the client message |
| R4-2.3 | "Crews, similar to clans or groups" | New module behind flag `crews` (on local/staging). A crew: name (3–40, unique, keyword rules), slug, description (≤ 500), logo + cover through the media pipeline, optional Bundesland, join mode `open` / `request` / `invite`. Roles founder · admin · member; a member is in at most 5 crews and founds at most 2. Crew page (app CREW-01): header, about, members with their public cars, join/leave/request, share link `/app/crews/{slug}`; crew list in Entdecken (CREW-00: search, Bundesland filter, alphabetical/newest — **never ranked by size**); "Meine Crews" on the own profile; a crew chip on the member profile. Founder/admin: edit, approve requests, invite by username, remove members, hand over founder, delete crew. Reportable (DSA pipeline), staff hide/delete with audit (ADM-80/81). No crew chat in this round (messages stay 1:1) |
