# DECISIONS — Architecture Decision Records

Decisions below are **final** for V1. The team does not re-open them; if reality forces a change (a package is incompatible, a spike fails), add a new ADR (`ADR-1xx`) with context → decision → consequences, and note it in HANDOFF. ADR-0xx were set by the team lead. **Phase 4 (SPEED CLUB 2.0) decisions are ADR-401 and up; where they conflict with an earlier ADR, the Phase 4 ADR and `docs/09_PREMIUM_2_0.md` win (ADR-401).**

| 134 | **Code guessing budget per account and purpose:** 6 digits (1,000,000 values), 5 attempts per code, 5 codes per hour, **10 codes per rolling 24 hours** (`max_codes_per_day`), so at most 25 guesses an hour and **50 in 24 hours**: 0.005 % per day, about 1.8 % over a year of sustained attack, during which the owner gets 10 mails a day.
