---
title: Blocking and reporting
sort: 60
---
**Blocking:** on every profile, you'll find **Block** under **More**. After that you no longer see each other's profiles, vehicles and messages, and you no longer follow each other. The other person is not told. You manage blocked accounts in **Settings › Privacy › Blocked accounts**.

**Reporting:** if a profile, vehicle, photo or message breaks the community guidelines or the law, tap **Report** and choose a reason. Our team reviews every report. You can see what happened to your report in **Settings › Privacy › Reports & decisions**.

If someone's life or health is at risk, please call the police (110 in Germany) right away.
