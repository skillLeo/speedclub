---
title: Exporting my data
sort: 70
---
You can request a copy of your data at any time: **Settings › Data › Export my data**.

You get a ZIP file with:
- your profile, settings and consents,
- every vehicle with specs, modifications and history,
- the originals of all photos you uploaded,
- follows, blocks, saved builds, conversations, notifications, reports and support requests.

The file is usually ready within a few minutes. We send you a link by e-mail that works for 7 days. After that we delete the file. You can request one export every 24 hours.
