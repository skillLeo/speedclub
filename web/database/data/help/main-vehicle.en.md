---
title: What is the main vehicle?
sort: 30
---
Your main vehicle sits at the top of your profile and on your profile card. It is the car others connect with you first.

To set it: open the vehicle in the garage and tap **Set as main vehicle**. There is only ever one.

If you delete your main vehicle, the next published vehicle takes over automatically, public ones first. You change the order of the other vehicles in the garage by dragging.
