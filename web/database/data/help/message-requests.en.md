---
title: Messages and requests
sort: 50
---
If you write to someone who does not follow you, your first message lands in their **Requests**. Only when they accept or reply does it become a normal conversation. Until then you cannot send another message.

If someone follows you, your messages go straight to their conversations.

In **Settings › Privacy & contact › Who can message you** you choose:
- **Everyone:** any member can write to you or send a request.
- **Members I follow:** others cannot start a new conversation.
- **Nobody:** no new conversations or requests. Existing conversations stay open.

You can also turn read receipts off there. Then you no longer see whether others have read your messages either.
