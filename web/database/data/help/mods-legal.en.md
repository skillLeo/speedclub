---
title: Modifications and registration
sort: 40
---
Many parts need an ABE, a parts certificate (Teilegutachten) or a registration under StVZO §§ 19/21. Only you know whether that is done for your car.

When you add a modification you can therefore optionally state its status (for example "registered" or "ABE available"). This always comes from you and is labelled that way everywhere: **"Stated by the owner, no legal review."**

SPEED CLUB does not check legality and never says that a modification is allowed. If in doubt, an inspection body (TÜV, DEKRA, GTÜ) or your registration office can help.

Illegal races are a criminal offence (§ 315d StGB). Content that calls for or glorifies them is not allowed in the community.
