---
title: Appearance: Carbon, Black or Light
sort: 100
---
SPEED CLUB comes in three appearances:
- **Carbon:** dark grey, the default.
- **Black (OLED):** deep black, ideal for OLED screens and at night.
- **Light:** easy to read in daylight.

You choose under **Settings › App › Appearance**. With **System**, the app follows your device's setting. Your choice applies in the app and in the browser once you are signed in.

Under **Settings › App › Language & units** you set the language and the power display, for example "510 PS (375 kW)" or "375 kW (510 PS)".
