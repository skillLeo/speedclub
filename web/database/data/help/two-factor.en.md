---
title: Two-factor protection
sort: 90
---
With two-factor protection, you need a code from an authenticator app (for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password) in addition to your password when you sign in.

To turn it on: **Settings › Security › Two-factor protection**. Scan the QR code with your app or enter the key manually, then confirm with the first code.

You then get 10 **recovery codes**. Keep them somewhere safe, for example printed out or in a password manager. If you lose your phone, you can sign in with them. Each code works only once. You can create new codes at any time; the old ones then stop working.

Resetting your password does not turn two-factor protection off.
