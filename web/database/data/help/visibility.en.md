---
title: Who sees my vehicles?
sort: 20
---
Every vehicle has its own visibility. You set it when you publish and can change it in the garage at any time.

- **Public:** everybody sees the build, even without an account. It appears in Discover, in search and in the feed.
- **Unlisted:** whoever has the link or the QR code sees the build. It does not show up in lists or search.
- **Private:** only you see it. The QR code then shows "private".
- **Draft:** not published yet, only for you.

In **Settings › Privacy & contact** you also decide whether you appear in member search, whether search engines may find your profile and builds, and whether QR pages show your name.

If you block someone, you no longer see each other's profiles and vehicles.
