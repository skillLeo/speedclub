# Community guidelines

SPEED CLUB lives on people who love their cars and talk about them openly. These rules keep it that way.

## 1. Cars first

Show your build and stick to the facts. Enter specs as they are, and be honest about whether a power figure is factory, your own claim or measured.

## 2. Respect

No harassment, hate or threats. Criticising a car is fine, attacking people is not. Do not publish other people's private data.

## 3. Legal only

Illegal street races are a criminal offence (§ 315d StGB). Do not organise, promote or glorify them, including through videos or meeting points. No instructions for evading inspections or the police.

Our underground look is an aesthetic only. Driving hard belongs on the track, not in town.

## 4. Your content

Only upload photos you hold the rights to. No nudity and no depictions of violence.

## 5. Privacy

Think about number plates and people in your photos. Whether you show your plate is your choice. Where available, our tool helps you obscure it.

## 6. No spam

No mass messages and no undeclared advertising. If you are paid for a part or got it for free, say so.

## 7. Modifications and the law

Modification details are the owner's statements. SPEED CLUB says nothing about whether a modification is permitted. The legal status field therefore always reads: owner's statement, not a legal review.

## 8. Reporting

If you see something that does not belong here, report it: in the app on any member, vehicle, photo or message. Without an account, use our report form at /melden or write to us at [[Melde-E-Mail]]. For illegal content we need an explanation, the location (URL) and your statement that you are acting in good faith. If you give an email address in the form, we confirm receipt and tell you our decision. Whoever reported something learns the outcome and can appeal against it within **6 months**. Moderators only see the reported messages and the context needed.

## 9. Consequences

Depending on severity and frequency, we remove content, restrict features or suspend accounts. You always get a reason and can appeal within **6 months**. A different person decides the appeal.
