# Community guidelines

Speedclub lives on people who love their cars and talk about them openly. These rules keep it that way.

## 1. Cars first

Show your build and stick to the facts. Enter specs as they are, and be honest about whether a power figure is factory, your own claim or measured.

## 2. Respect

No harassment, hate or threats. Criticising a car is fine, attacking people is not. Do not publish other people's private data.

## 3. Legal only

Illegal street races are a criminal offence (§ 315d StGB). Do not organise, promote or glorify them, including through videos or meeting points. No instructions for evading inspections or the police.

Our underground look is an aesthetic only. Driving hard belongs on the track, not in town.

## 4. Your content

Only upload photos you hold the rights to. No nudity and no depictions of violence.

## 5. Privacy

Think about number plates and people in your photos. Whether you show your plate is your choice. When you upload, "Make number plates unreadable" is on; you mark the spot and we pixelate it.

## 6. No spam

No mass messages and no undeclared advertising. If you are paid for a part or got it for free, say so.

## 7. Modifications and the law

Modification details are the owner's statements. Speedclub says nothing about whether a modification is permitted. The legal status field therefore always reads: owner's statement, not a legal review.

## 8. Meets

Meets on Speedclub are legal meets. Whoever organises one confirms these rules, and they apply just as much to everyone who comes:

- Legal only: no racing, no dangerous driving on public roads.
- No burnouts, no drifting, no racing. That belongs on the track, not in the car park.
- Respect residents: engines off, no loud music.
- Leave no rubbish behind.
- Permits and the venue owner's consent are in place. Without the owner's permission, a car park is not a meeting point.

Give honest details of time, place and schedule, and cancel in good time if the meet is off. If the place is visible only to members who are going, do not share it publicly. No meets that are only a cover for races or show-off driving.

## 9. Businesses and advertising

Businesses show their work on Speedclub. Only tag a business with "Installed by" if it really did the work, and as a business only tag builds you worked on. Keep your imprint up to date. No bought or invented recommendations, no advertising in messages to members who did not ask for it, and always label advertising as such.

## 10. Reactions, mentions and comments

A reaction is a quick signal to the owner, not a competition. Only mention members when it is about them or their car, not to nudge as many people as possible. Anyone who does not want to be mentioned can set that in their settings. Once comments exist, the same rules apply to them as to everything else here: criticising a car is fine, attacking people is not.

## 11. STREET DEX and spots

Spotting means: you see a car with a Speedclub sticker and scan it in person.

- Only scan stickers of cars you are standing in front of, no codes from photos, screenshots or the internet.
- Leave the car alone: do not touch it, sit on it or block it in.
- Do not follow a car to spot it.
- Do not follow anyone and do not lie in wait for the owner.
- Do not publish where a particular car is parked or when it was where.
- Do not photograph people at the car who do not want it.
- One account per person. No second accounts and no arranged or made-up spots.

Anyone who uses Ghost mode, "Pause spotting" or "Hide cities" wants to be found less. Respect that.

## 12. Levels, badges, Build Score and invites

XP, badges and the Build Score follow fixed rules everyone can see in the app. Do not game them: no made-up mods, no other people's photos, no mods you only delete and add again, no second accounts. Invites are for people you really want in, not for mass advertising (see section 6).

## 13. Reporting

If you see something that does not belong here, report it: in the app on any member, vehicle, photo, build update, comment, meet, business or message. Without an account, use our report form at /melden or write to us at [[Melde-E-Mail]]. For illegal content we need an explanation, the location (URL) and your statement that you are acting in good faith. If you give an email address in the form, we confirm receipt and tell you our decision. Whoever reported something learns the outcome and can appeal against it within **6 months**. Moderators only see the reported messages and the context needed.

## 14. Consequences

Depending on severity and frequency, we remove content or spots, take back XP, sets and badges, cancel meets, suspend business profiles, restrict features or suspend accounts. You always get a reason and can appeal within **6 months**. A different person decides the appeal.
