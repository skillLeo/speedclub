## 3. What is publicly visible

Speedclub is a community, so your profile and vehicles are public by default, including to people without an account and through the QR code on your car. You can change this:

- Set each vehicle to "Public", "Unlisted" or "Private".
- For each build update, choose whether everyone who may see the vehicle sees it, or only your followers.
- Decide whether search engines may find your profile.
- Decide who may message you, who may mention you, and turn read receipts off.

Reactions are private: only the owner of a build or update sees how many reactions it got and from whom. Others only see their own reaction.

View counts and statistics (section 18) are seen by the owner only, and nobody sees who viewed. Only a goal you set for a build is public ("Goal: season 2027").

The forum (section 19) can be read by people without an account too. Your username and profile picture appear with your topics and replies. How often a reply was marked "helpful" is visible in the forum only; nobody sees who marked it.

For meets, the number of members going is public. Signed-in members see who is going, as long as that person is discoverable in their settings; the organiser sees every answer. Organisers can show the exact address to members who are going only; everyone else then sees just the city and an approximate area.

A business profile is public, with its imprint. An "Installed by" tag appears only once the member and the business have both agreed; either side can remove it again.

Your level with its title appears next to your name, your XP does not. Your earned badges and, if you have one, your Founding Member number are public on your profile. Badges such as "Street Famous" therefore show that at least 10, 50, 100 or 500 people have spotted one of your cars.

For a car, these are public: its Dex number, its rarity, its Build Score with stage and ranks (only you as the owner see the points per part) and, while it is Car of the Day, its story line on Home.

How often and by whom a car was spotted is seen only by its owner. Signed-in members only see on the Dex card in how many cities a car was spotted, as long as the owner does not hide the cities. In Ghost mode a car does not show up in search, Discover, lists, your public garage or the spot map. Its page stays reachable through the link and the sticker, and search engines are asked not to index it.

## 4. Photos, number plates and faces

Every photo is re-saved on upload, which removes metadata such as GPS position and camera data. Number plates and faces can be personal data, so please think about them before uploading. The decision is yours.

**Make number plates unreadable.** When you upload, the switch "Make number plates unreadable" is on. You mark the spot, and our server pixelates it before it stores the photo. We only store the pixelated version, also as the private original file; the unpixelated photo exists only during processing. Every size, preview image, share card and the Season Wrapped cards are made from the pixelated version. This applies to photos of vehicles, mods, build updates and messages, to your avatar and cover photo, and to images of meets and businesses. If you turn the switch off, we store the photo without pixelation and remember your decision. For photos of your vehicles, mods, build updates and profile you can still pixelate later; we then replace the private original file too. You can only undo this by uploading the photo again. For photos you uploaded before, we ask you once in the garage ("Check number plates"). We change nothing without you. Our tool does not detect number plates or faces by itself; it only suggests an area that you move.

Photos on maintenance entries are private and only available to you through time-limited links.

## 5. QR codes

When someone scans your QR code, we only increase a daily counter for that code. Scans by known bots do not count, and yours do not count only when you are signed in on the same browser. We store neither an IP address nor a user account with the counter. We process the IP address only briefly during the request, to limit abuse.

## 6. Sign-in and account security

**Ways to sign in.** You sign in with a code we email you, or with the link in the same email. We store the code and the link only as checksums, and your email address in this context too. A code is valid for 10 minutes. You can also use passkeys, Sign in with Apple or Google, and a password.

**Passkeys.** A passkey stays on your device or in your password manager. We only store the public key. Your device checks your fingerprint, face or device PIN itself; we receive none of it.

**Apple and Google.** When you sign in with Apple or Google, you first sign in with that provider. It sends us a signed confirmation with your identifier and email address, which we verify. With Apple you can hide your address; we then receive an Apple relay address. We only link an address that already belongs to an account after you confirm a code sent to that address. Apple's and Google's own privacy notices apply to their processing. When you unlink the provider or delete your account, we delete the link. When you delete your account, we also revoke Sign in with Apple with Apple.

**Devices and security activity.** In settings you see where you are signed in (device, platform, app version, last use) and can end sessions. Under "Security activity" you see new sign-ins, passkeys added or removed, providers linked or unlinked, email changes and failed sign-ins (as one entry per day). We store no IP address for this. **Warnings.** We always email you about a sign-in from a new device and about important changes to your account; this cannot be turned off. **App lock.** The app lock with Face ID, fingerprint or device passcode runs only on your device. We learn nothing about it.

## 7. Meets

Organisers publish meets with a time and place. We review an organiser's first meets, and every meet whose place is visible only to members who are going, before they appear. Organisers must confirm that their meet is legal.

When you say you are going, interested or join the waitlist, the organiser sees your answer with your username and its time. The organiser can send you updates and take you off the list. We remind members who are going 24 hours and 2 hours before the start; you only get email reminders if you turned them on, and they never name the exact place.

**Calendar.** A meet's calendar file (.ics) contains only what is public. Members who are going get a personal calendar link with the exact place; it works until 2 days after the meet.

**Location.** For "near you" you use either the region from your settings or, in the web app, your browser's location once, if you allow it. We round the device location to about one kilometre, send it only with that search and do not store it.

**New meets near you.** Only if you turn it on under notifications in settings (it is off by default), we send you at most once a week, on Thursdays from 6 pm, a notification or email about new public meets in the next 14 days within 50 km of the region from your settings. We only use this region you chose, never your device's location. Without a region or without new meets we send nothing. Every one of these emails has a link that unsubscribes you; mail apps that offer one-click unsubscribe use the same way.

**Photos after the meet.** The organiser and everyone who is going or checked in can add photos to a meet's gallery from its end until 14 days later; while the meet is running, the gallery is closed. Only the organiser sees attendees' photos until they approve them; if they reject a photo, only you see the reason, and we delete the image files after 7 days. The "Make number plates unreadable" switch applies to these photos like to all others. You can delete your photos at any time.

**Check-in.** At the meet you can scan or type a code from the organiser's phone. We only store that you checked in and when, never a location, a device or an IP address. "War dabei" ("Was there") appears on your profile only once the meet is over, and only as long as you do not turn it off; the organiser only sees the number of check-ins. You can remove a check-in at any time.

## 8. Maps and place search

**Maps** load only when you tap "Show map". Your device then loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap, which receives your IP address and the map areas. We serve the map software itself from our own server. The map data comes from OpenStreetMap ("© OpenStreetMap contributors"). "Route" and "Open in map" take you to openstreetmap.org, where its own privacy notice applies.

**Place search.** When an organiser or you search for a place, our server asks Nominatim, the search service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Nominatim only receives the search term from our server, not your IP address or account. We cache search terms and results for 30 days, without any link to you.

We count profile views only as a daily number, without visitor, address or cookie.

**Parts catalogue.** The catalogue lists parts with the manufacturers' details. If you link a part to your modification, it becomes part of the build.

**Advertising.** Once we turn on partner links, we label them "Ad". If you tap one, you leave Speedclub. We do not count these clicks. ## 10. Web app on the home screen and browser push

You can install the web app on your home screen. A service worker then stores the app's files (program code, fonts, icons) on your device, so it starts faster and shows a notice page when offline. It does not store our server's responses that contain your data. It collects no usage data.

**Browser push.** When you turn on notifications in the web app and your browser asks whether to allow them, your browser creates an address with its push service. We store this address encrypted. Depending on the browser, the push service of Google (for example Chrome), Mozilla (Firefox), Apple (Safari) or Microsoft (Edge on Windows) delivers the notifications. The content is end-to-end encrypted; the push service only sees that a notification goes to your browser, and keeps it for at most 12 hours if the browser is offline. We identify ourselves to the push service with our own key (VAPID). You choose which notifications you get, as in the app; message previews only appear if you turned them on. You can turn browser push off at any time in the web app or in your browser.

## 11. Preview images for shared links

For public vehicles, profiles, meets, businesses and brands we create preview images that messengers and social networks show when someone shares a link. They only show what is public anyway. If content becomes private, hidden or deleted, we remove its preview image immediately; if anything else changes, we clean up once a day. Copies another service already loaded when the link was shared are outside our control. If an image our server draws shows a QR code (preview images, share cards, Season Wrapped cards), it leads to the car's public page and never to the code of your sticker.

## 12. App error reports

When the app hits an error, it sends us a report: which screen, which error message, the technical trace, platform, app version, language, font size, window size and browser identifier, and your member ID if you are signed in. Nothing you typed is included. We use no external service for this.

## 13. STREET DEX: camera, location and spots

With STREET DEX you collect other members' cars: in the app you scan the sticker of a car that is in front of you.

**Camera.** The app uses the camera only to read the code on the sticker. We store no photos and no videos, and the microphone stays off. The web app has no scanner.

**Location.** The app asks for your location only when you scan, and only if you allow it. Without permission we store the spot without a city. The app only asks for an approximate location and never in the background: on Android only the coarse location, on iOS with reduced accuracy. With it, your device looks up the nearest city in a list of places the app loads from our server (data by GeoNames). Only that city and its federal state reach us, never coordinates. The app does not store the location. Your device's operating system determines the location; Apple's or Google's privacy notices apply to this. In the web app we do not ask for your location.

**What we store.** For each spot: which car, who, when, city and federal state if known, whether it was between 10 pm and 6 am, and whether you were among the first three spotters. We do not store coordinates. The same car counts for you at most once in 24 hours. Spots from accounts younger than 24 hours and spots of sample cars are stored, but they do not count.

**What the owner sees.** The owner sees how often and by how many people their car was spotted, in which cities, on which of the last 30 days and who spotted it most recently (up to 10 spotters). Names and days appear 3 hours after the spot at the earliest. The owner sees your username only if "Show my spots" is on, your profile is publicly visible and neither of you has blocked the other; otherwise they only see "someone". "Show my spots" is on by default; you can turn it off in settings. [[check whether "Show my spots" must be off by default under Art. 25(2) GDPR]] We never name the city of a spot between 10 pm and 6 am for a single car, neither to the owner nor in Season Wrapped. Every 3 hours we combine new spots of a car into one notification per day; it never says who spotted.

**Your car's settings.** In Ghost mode your car no longer shows up in search, Discover and lists; only someone who scans the sticker in person finds it. "Pause spotting" stops new spots; earlier ones stay. "Hide cities" shows no city for your car any more, not even in your notifications.

**Spot map.** The spot map in Discover only shows cities and their number of spots, never cars or people. It counts spots that are at least 24 hours and at most 90 days old, and shows a city only if its spots come from at least 5 different cars. Cars in Ghost mode or with hidden cities do not count.

**Spotting abuse prevention.** So that nobody collects with second accounts or made-up spots, we show our team signals: more than 10 spots by one account in 10 minutes, spots in two cities more than 300 km apart within one hour, and several spotting accounts on the same app install. We calculate these signals when they are viewed and do not store them. For the last signal we store for each app install a second encrypted checksum of its identifier, without any link to your account. Only this check reads it. The team only sees which accounts are affected, never the checksum. It appears in no answer to the app, in no data export and on no page, and it is deleted with the app install. A signal never leads to an automatic block; a human always decides. If the team removes a spot, you lose the XP and completed sets that rest on it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in fair spots (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 14. Levels, badges, Founding Member and Car of the Day

**XP and levels.** You earn XP by fixed rules, for example for your first published car, documented mods, one build update a day, a newly spotted car or a completed set. There is no XP for likes and reactions, for followers, for the number of your posts or for signing in. At most 200 XP are possible per day. Your XP give your level from 1 to 10 with a title. The app shows the rules.

**Badges.** Badges also follow fixed rules, for example for documented mods, spotted cars, sets, an active sticker or your Build Score. No badge counts likes, reactions, followers, posts or sign-ins. Some badges stay hidden until you have them. In case of abuse our team can take a badge back.

**Founding Member.** The first 1,000 members get a running number from #0001 to #1000 and the "Founding Member" badge when their account is activated (confirmed email address and acceptance of the current terms). Anyone who was already active when we introduced the numbers gets the next free number in the order of activation. A number is never given out again, not even after an account is deleted. Test and sample accounts get none.

**Car of the Day.** Every day at 6 pm we show one car as Car of the Day on Home, until 6 pm the next day. We pick it by fixed rules, never at random: a public car with a Build Score of at least 400 and at least 4 photos that was not picked in the last 180 days. The federal state, which comes from the owner's profile city, rotates; within it the highest Build Score decides. Our team can change or skip a day. As the story line we use the first sentence of your description. While your car is Car of the Day you can change it (140 characters at most). We tell you when your car is picked. Other members only get a notification about it if they turned that on. If you do not want to be picked, turn on Ghost mode or set the car to "Unlisted" or "Private". We then do not pick it, and on its day it disappears from Home at once.

## 15. Invites

You have 5 invite codes. Anyone who opens your invite link sees your username and display name. If someone registers with your code, we store the code with their account. As soon as the account is activated, we link the code to it and store when that happened. You then see who used your code: with their username if the member is publicly visible and neither of you has blocked the other, otherwise as "a member". You get a notification about it without a name. The invited person sees who is inviting them when they register. The data export shows both sides who the code is linked to, by the same rule. Both get only the "Crew Starter" badge, no XP and no other benefits. Codes of deleted accounts stop working.

## 16. Build Score

The Build Score (0 to 1,000) shows how complete and well documented a build is. It comes from fixed rules in four parts: completeness (photos, filled-in specs, description, crop of the cover photo), documentation (mods with brand, install date and photo; mods you state as registered), community (how many different members reacted, spotted or followed) and history (your entries, before/after pairs, the time span of your documentation). What counts is whether a field is filled in, never its value: power, torque and costs are not used. The Build Score does not show who reacted, spotted or followed.

The Build Score, stage and ranks are public. Only you as the owner see the points per part and the last change ("+25"), because otherwise they would show how many members reacted. Ranks exist only in groups of at least 3 public cars: by generation in Germany and by federal state. We take the federal state only from the city in your public profile, never from the region you set for "near you". Without a city in your profile there is no rank by federal state. Cars in Ghost mode, unlisted and private cars get no rank. We store the score for every day with a change, for Season Wrapped and your data export. Only you see the suggestions for the next upgrade; community points never appear there.

## 17. Season Wrapped

Season Wrapped is your recap of the season (1 March to 31 October) and of the year. We create it from 7 days before the date we set and announce in the app, and only if you were active in the period. It consists of your own data only:

- your spots: cities only, at most 5, no cities of spots between 10 pm and 6 am or of cars with hidden cities, no cars in Ghost mode and no owners,
- the scans of your stickers and the spots of your cars,
- how your Build Score developed,
- one mod or one before/after from the period,
- meets you said you were going to; titles only of public meets,
- your build type, derived from your mods,
- your place in your federal state as a percentage, only if at least 20 members are compared and you are in the top 50 %.

No other member's name appears in it. It only shows photos of your own cars: the cover and further photos from the gallery or of installed mods of the car the card is about, and your before/after. We never use photos of other members, their cars or their meets. We only use a photo for which you made a number plate decision, that is pixelated or deliberately not.

Only you see your recap. The card images are kept in non-public storage. If you share the last card, its QR code leads to your car's public page, never to the code of your sticker. We count how often you opened "Share" and remember when you first viewed the recap. Season Wrapped is available once we turn it on.

## 18. Views and statistics

We count how often public vehicle pages and profiles are viewed, so that owners see how their build is received. We count one view per person, page and calendar day (German time), together with the source: app, web, sticker scan or shared link. We recognise a shared link by the ending "?via=share" that the app adds when you share.

So that we do not count a person twice on one day, we make a checksum from your member number – or, if you are not signed in, from your IP address (for IPv6 shortened to the first 64 bits) and the identifier of your browser or app – with a secret key that is valid for that day only. We store only this checksum, the page, the day and the source: no time of day, no IP address, no browser identifier and no member number. We delete the day's key shortly after midnight; after that nobody – not even we – can link a stored checksum to a person. From one address we count at most 3 new views per page and day, so that the numbers cannot be pushed up artificially. We delete the single entries after 90 days; only the daily totals remain.

We do not count views of your own pages, views by our team in the support view, known bots, preview services and browser prefetches, sample content and pages you may not see (for example after a block).

In "Statistics" owners see totals only: views per day and week, the sources, sticker scans, spots, new followers, how often the build was saved and the reactions. **We show nobody who viewed a page** – not the owners, not other members and not our team, not even in the support view. View counts are never public. From 100 views the "100 views" badge can appear on the profile; it names no number.

We store nothing on your device for this (no cookies, no local storage). The legal basis is our and the owners' legitimate interest in data-minimising reach statistics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). [[Prüfen: § 25 TDDDG nicht berührt, da kein Zugriff auf das Endgerät über die übliche Seitenanfrage hinaus]] Our team can switch the counting off completely; then we store nothing at all.

## 19. Forum

In the forum you ask questions, share knowledge and help others. Anyone can read it, also without an account; writing, marking replies "helpful" and following topics need an account. We store your topics and replies with text, photos, tags and the vehicle, mod or part you link, your mentions, your "helpful" marks and the topics you follow. For search we build a list of search terms from the title, text, tags, category and linked vehicle and store it with the topic.

If your account is younger than 3 days or your email address is not confirmed yet, our team reviews new topics and replies before others see them (pre-moderation); until then only you see them. If we reject a post, you see the reason. We handle reported posts under the DSA notice-and-action procedure (section 2, "Reports and moderation").

How often a reply was marked "helpful" is visible to everyone in the forum, nowhere else; nobody sees who marked it. If you delete your account, we delete your posts with their texts, your marks and the topics you follow. A topic you started in which others replied keeps its title and the others' replies, without your name and without your text [[Rechtliche Prüfung: Titel bleiben nach Löschung des Kontos (OA-63)]]; a topic without replies from others is deleted completely.

The legal basis is the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), for moderation our legal obligations and our legitimate interest in a safe community (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR).

## 23. AI features

AI features are only active if we have turned them on.

**Features for you:** You trigger search, suggestions for mods and descriptions, and translations yourself. **Manufacturer figures and parts:** If you choose make, model and generation in the vehicle wizard, AI can suggest typical manufacturer figures ("Take over figures?"). **Summaries in the forum:** Forum topics with more than 20 replies can show a short three-line summary, labelled "AI summary". For this we send the title and the text of public posts to the AI provider, without names, mentions, links and photos. If you have blocked a member whose post went into a summary, or they have blocked you, we do not show it to you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a clear forum (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). ## 25. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. We reply within one month.

- **Correct your data:** Directly in your profile, garage, meets and business profile. We review the imprint details of a verified business again after a change.
- **Settings:** Who may mention you and message you, notifications per type and channel, quiet hours, browser push, your region, your devices' sessions, "Show my spots", build reminders and the weekly recap and, for each car, Ghost mode, "Pause spotting" and "Hide cities".
