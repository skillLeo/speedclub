## 3. What is publicly visible

SPEED CLUB is a community, so your profile and vehicles are public by default, including to people without an account and through the QR code on your car. You can change this:

- Set each vehicle to "Public", "Unlisted" or "Private".
- For each build update, choose whether everyone who may see the vehicle sees it, or only your followers.
- Decide whether search engines may find your profile.
- Decide who may message you, who may mention you, and turn read receipts off.

Reactions are private: only the owner of a build or update sees how many reactions it got and from whom. Others only see their own reaction.

For meets, the number of members going is public. Signed-in members see who is going, as long as that person is discoverable in their settings; the organiser sees every answer. Organisers can show the exact address to members who are going only; everyone else then sees just the city and an approximate area.

A business profile is public, with its imprint. An "Installed by" tag appears only once the member and the business have both agreed; either side can remove it again.

## 4. Photos, number plates and faces

Every photo is re-saved on upload, which removes metadata such as GPS position and camera data. Number plates and faces can be personal data, so please think about them before uploading. Where available, you can obscure them with our tool. The decision is yours. Photos on maintenance entries are private and only available to you through time-limited links.

## 5. QR codes

When someone scans your QR code, we only increase a daily counter for that code. Scans by known bots do not count, and yours do not count only when you are signed in on the same browser. We store neither an IP address nor a user account with the counter. We process the IP address only briefly during the request, to limit abuse.

## 6. Sign-in and account security

**Ways to sign in.** You sign in with a code we email you, or with the link in the same email. We store the code and the link only as checksums, and your email address in this context too. A code is valid for 10 minutes. You can also use passkeys, Sign in with Apple or Google, and a password.

**Passkeys.** A passkey stays on your device or in your password manager. We only store the public key. Your device checks your fingerprint, face or device PIN itself; we receive none of it.

**Apple and Google.** When you sign in with Apple or Google, you first sign in with that provider. It sends us a signed confirmation with your identifier and email address, which we verify. With Apple you can hide your address; we then receive an Apple relay address. We only link an address that already belongs to an account after you confirm a code sent to that address. Apple's and Google's own privacy notices apply to their processing. When you unlink the provider or delete your account, we delete the link. When you delete your account, we also revoke Sign in with Apple with Apple.

**Devices and security activity.** In settings you see where you are signed in (device, platform, app version, last use) and can end sessions. Under "Security activity" you see new sign-ins, passkeys added or removed, providers linked or unlinked, email changes and failed sign-ins (as one entry per day). We store no IP address for this. **Warnings.** We always email you about a sign-in from a new device and about important changes to your account; this cannot be turned off. The "This wasn't me" link in these emails works for 7 days and only once: it signs out every device, removes ways to sign in added since the reported event, and opens a support request for you.

**App lock.** The app lock with Face ID, fingerprint or device passcode runs only on your device. We learn nothing about it.

## 7. Meets

Organisers publish meets with a time and place. We review an organiser's first meets, and every meet whose place is visible only to members who are going, before they appear. Organisers must confirm that their meet is legal.

When you say you are going, interested or join the waitlist, the organiser sees your answer with your username and its time. The organiser can send you updates and take you off the list. We remind members who are going 24 hours and 2 hours before the start; you only get email reminders if you turned them on, and they never name the exact place.

**Calendar.** A meet's calendar file (.ics) contains only what is public. Members who are going get a personal calendar link with the exact place; it works until 2 days after the meet.

**Location.** For "near you" you use either the region from your settings or, in the web app, your browser's location once, if you allow it. We round the device location to about one kilometre, send it only with that search and do not store it.

## 8. Maps and place search

**Maps** load only when you tap "Show map". Your device then loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap, which receives your IP address and the map areas. We serve the map software itself from our own server. The map data comes from OpenStreetMap ("© OpenStreetMap contributors"). "Route" and "Open in map" take you to openstreetmap.org, where its own privacy notice applies.

**Place search.** When an organiser or you search for a place, our server asks Nominatim, the search service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Nominatim only receives the search term from our server, not your IP address or account. We cache search terms and results for 30 days, without any link to you.

## 9. Businesses, parts catalogue and advertising

**Businesses.** Workshops, tuners and other businesses can apply for a profile. We review the application, that is the name, address, imprint details and whether the website or register entry is plausible, and decide with a reason. The business profile and imprint are public. The business itself is responsible for the details in its imprint. Team members join only through an invitation the member accepts. We count profile views only as a daily number, without visitor, address or cookie.

**Parts catalogue.** The catalogue lists parts with the manufacturers' details. If you link a part to your modification, it becomes part of the build.

**Advertising.** Once we turn on partner links, we label them "Ad". If you tap one, you leave SPEED CLUB. We do not count these clicks. The shop and the partner programme may record that you came from SPEED CLUB and pay us a commission; their privacy notices apply. ## 10. Web app on the home screen and browser push

You can install the web app on your home screen. A service worker then stores the app's files (program code, fonts, icons) on your device, so it starts faster and shows a notice page when offline. It does not store our server's responses that contain your data. It collects no usage data.

**Browser push.** When you turn on notifications in the web app and your browser asks whether to allow them, your browser creates an address with its push service. We store this address encrypted. Depending on the browser, the push service of Google (for example Chrome), Mozilla (Firefox), Apple (Safari) or Microsoft (Edge on Windows) delivers the notifications. The content is end-to-end encrypted; the push service only sees that a notification goes to your browser, and keeps it for at most 12 hours if the browser is offline. We identify ourselves to the push service with our own key (VAPID). You choose which notifications you get, as in the app; message previews only appear if you turned them on. You can turn browser push off at any time in the web app or in your browser.

## 11. Preview images for shared links

For public vehicles, profiles, meets, businesses and brands we create preview images that messengers and social networks show when someone shares a link. They only show what is public anyway. If content becomes private, hidden or deleted, we remove its preview image immediately; if anything else changes, we clean up once a day. Copies another service already loaded when the link was shared are outside our control.

## 12. App error reports

When the app hits an error, it sends us a report: which screen, which error message, the technical trace, platform, app version, language, font size, window size and browser identifier, and your member ID if you are signed in. Nothing you typed is included. We use no external service for this.

## 13. Cookies and storage on your device

We only use strictly necessary storage under § 25(2) TDDDG:

- in the web app, the session and form protection (XSRF); the public pages (home, QR, vehicle, member, meet and business pages, legal texts) set no cookies for guests,
- in the web app after sign-in, a random identifier of your browser (cookie "sc_browser", 5 years), so we can warn you about a sign-in from a new browser [[check whether this falls under § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG and whether the lifetime is appropriate]],
- your sign-in token on the device,
- your appearance, language, unit and app lock settings,
- the list of your recent searches, which stays on your device only and which you can delete in "Discover" at any time,
- an offline cache of recently loaded content (for example your garage, the home feed, notifications, your list of conversations and your settings), so the app works without a connection; we clear it when you sign out,
- drafts of long forms (for example vehicle, modification, build update, meet) and a queue of posts and messages you created offline, until they are sent,
- the queue for photo uploads, so an upload survives an app restart,
- on Android, the "My car" widget with your vehicle's photo and next due item, if you add it, and local reminders of due items,
- in the installed web app, the app's files in the service worker, the browser push registration if you allow it, and whether we have shown you the install hint,
- map tiles your browser caches after you tap a map,
- in the report form (/melden), the contact form (/kontakt) and the account deletion form (/konto-loeschen), Cloudflare Turnstile for abuse prevention, if we have turned it on. The report form itself sets no cookie; we process your IP address there only briefly for rate limiting.

Staff have a separate session in the admin area. That is why there is no cookie banner. We use no analytics and no advertising services.

## 15. AI features

AI features are only active if we have turned them on.

**Features for you:** You trigger search, suggestions for mods and descriptions, and translations yourself. When you upload photos, AI can suggest a category, and the photo is sent to the AI provider for this. We label all of this "AI suggestion", "AI-assisted search" or "Translated (AI)". Nothing is published without your confirmation. Before first use we explain what data is processed. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

**Internal pre-sorting:** If we have turned it on, AI pre-sorts incoming reports and photos and suggests a priority with a short reason to our team. You do not trigger this yourself, and the AI provider receives the reported content for it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in effective moderation and our DSA obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR). A human always decides, and the statement of reasons says whether automated means were involved.

AI never judges whether a modification is legally permitted.

## 17. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. We reply within one month.

You can do much of this yourself:

- **Export your data:** In settings you get a ZIP archive with your data and photos, including build updates, reactions, collections, meets, maintenance, your business, devices and your security activity.
- **Delete your account:** In the app or at /konto-loeschen. Your account is deactivated immediately and finally deleted within 30 days. We remove passkeys, links to Apple or Google and browser push immediately.
- **Correct your data:** Directly in your profile, garage, meets and business profile. We review the imprint details of a verified business again after a change.
- **Settings:** Who may mention you and message you, notifications per type and channel, quiet hours, browser push, your region and your devices' sessions.

For anything else, write to [[Datenschutz-E-Mail]]. You can also complain to a supervisory authority, for example [[Zuständige Aufsichtsbehörde]].
