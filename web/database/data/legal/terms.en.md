## 2. What SPEED CLUB is

SPEED CLUB is a community for car enthusiasts. You document your vehicles and modifications in a digital garage, discover builds of other members, message them, and get a QR code for every published vehicle that leads to its public page.

## 8. Moderation, restrictions and complaints

In the app, every member can report content and other members. Without an account, you can do so through our report form at /melden or by email at [[Melde-E-Mail]]. Whoever sends a report learns how we decided. Reports are reviewed by people; automated tools are used at most for pre-sorting. We tell you if automated means were used.

If rules are broken, we can remove or hide content, restrict messaging or publishing, or suspend your account temporarily or permanently. We act proportionately and take severity and frequency into account.

We inform you of every restriction with a statement of reasons under Art. 17 DSA: the facts, the ground (rule or law), any use of automated means, and your options to challenge it.

You can appeal free of charge within **6 months** of our notice, in the app or by email at [[Melde-E-Mail]]; this also applies if you reported something and disagree with our decision. An appeal is decided by a different person from the one who decided first. You can also turn to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body or to a court.
