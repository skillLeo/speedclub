## 2. What SPEED CLUB is

SPEED CLUB is a community for car enthusiasts. You document your vehicles and modifications in a digital garage, record progress in build updates, discover builds of other members, message them, and get a QR code for every published vehicle that leads to its public page. You can also find and organise meets, find workshops and other businesses, link your modifications to parts from our catalogue and get reminders for maintenance and the roadworthiness inspection.

SPEED CLUB is not a marketplace: no goods or services are sold through SPEED CLUB.

## 3. Account

- You must be at least 16 years old.
- Your details must be correct. You may only hold one account for yourself and must not pose as someone else.
- You sign in with an email code, a sign-in link, a passkey, with Apple or Google, or with a password. Keep your ways in secure, meaning your mailbox, your devices and, if you use one, your password. If you suspect someone has access, use "This wasn't me" in your security activity or in our warning email, and let us know.
- You can delete your account at any time in the app or at /konto-loeschen. It is deactivated immediately and finally deleted within 30 days. Your QR codes then stop working, your answers to meets are withdrawn and your upcoming meets are cancelled.

## 4. Your content

You keep ownership of your photos and texts. You grant us a non-exclusive, worldwide, free right to display them on SPEED CLUB, adapt them technically for that purpose, and use them in share and preview images of the vehicle, profile, meet or business concerned. This right ends when you delete the content or your account is deleted; backup periods are in the privacy policy.

You confirm that you hold the rights to your photos and that people shown have agreed. You can report copyright infringements through the "Report" function.

## 5. Vehicle and modification details

Specs and modifications come from members and are the owner's own statements. SPEED CLUB does not check them and says nothing about whether a modification is permitted. Many parts need an ABE, a parts certificate or a registration under §§ 19 and 21 StVZO. Clarifying this is your responsibility as the keeper.

The legal status field also only shows the owner's statement and is not a legal review.

## 6. QR stickers

The sticker placement guide is orientation, not legal advice. You are responsible for placing the sticker and for complying with the rules.

Our community guidelines apply. In particular, the following are forbidden:

- instructions for evading inspections or the police;
- harassment, hate, threats and publishing other people's private data;
- nudity, depictions of violence and other people's photos without permission;
- spam and undeclared advertising;
- automated scraping of the service and attacks on its security.

## 8. Build updates, reactions, mentions and comments

Build updates are your content within the meaning of section 4. For each update you decide who may see it. Reactions are private: only the owner of the build sees how many there are and who reacted. When you mention other members, they are notified if they allow mentions from you. We only turn comments on once we can moderate them; the same rules then apply to them.

## 9. Meets

**Who may organise meets.** You can publish meets if you have a confirmed email address, have been a member for at least 14 days and have accepted the rules for meets. We review each organiser's first meets, and every meet whose place is visible only to members who are going, before they appear. We may reject, hide or cancel a meet if it breaks these terms, the community guidelines or the law.

**The organiser's responsibility.** The organiser runs the meet, not SPEED CLUB. The organiser makes sure it is legal, that the necessary permits and the venue owner's consent are in place, and that the details of time, place and schedule are correct. If anything changes or the meet is cancelled, the organiser says so in good time on SPEED CLUB. [[Further organiser duties, such as insurance, noise control and site safety, and the allocation of liability for meets, to be set during the legal review]]

**Attending.** By saying you are going, you register with the organiser; there is no contract with SPEED CLUB about attending. The organiser sees your answer and can take you off the list. Where places are limited, the waitlist moves up in the order of answers. The number of members going is public. You are responsible for your own behaviour and vehicle at the meet and on the way there.

**Exact place.** If the organiser shows the place only to members who are going, you must not share it publicly.

## 10. Businesses

**Who may create a business profile.** Traders within the meaning of § 14 BGB can apply for a business profile, for example workshops, tuners, detailers, wrappers, paint shops, dealers and parts shops. The applicant must be authorised to act for the business. Each account can have one business profile. [[Whether businesses need supplementary terms for traders, to be set during the legal review]]

**Review.** We check the name, address, imprint details and whether the website or register entry is plausible, and decide with a reason. The "Verified" badge only means that we checked these details. It says nothing about the quality of the work. After verification, the imprint details can only be changed with a new review. We may review, suspend or remove a profile again if details are wrong or the business breaks these terms; two staff members decide a suspension together.

**Imprint.** Every business profile shows an imprint under § 5 DDG. The business is responsible for its accuracy and completeness.

**Team.** The owner can invite members to the team. They only join once they accept the invitation.

**"Installed by".** A business appears on a modification and in the business's portfolio only once the member and the business have both agreed. Either side can remove the tag again. Only tag work the business really did.

**No ratings.** SPEED CLUB shows no stars or ratings of businesses. Bought or invented recommendations are forbidden.

If you buy through one, the shop may pay us a commission. Any purchase contract is then only between you and the shop.

## 14. Moderation, restrictions and complaints

In the app, every member can report content and other members, including build updates, comments, meets and businesses. Without an account, you can do so through our report form at /melden or by email at [[Melde-E-Mail]]. Whoever sends a report learns how we decided. Reports are reviewed by people; automated tools are used at most for pre-sorting. We tell you if automated means were used.

If rules are broken, we can remove or hide content, cancel meets or suspend business profiles, restrict messaging or publishing, or suspend your account temporarily or permanently. We act proportionately and take severity and frequency into account.

We inform you of every restriction with a statement of reasons under Art. 17 DSA: the facts, the ground (rule or law), any use of automated means, and your options to challenge it. This also applies when we reject a meet or do not approve a business.

You can appeal free of charge within **6 months** of our notice, in the app or by email at [[Melde-E-Mail]]; this also applies if you reported something and disagree with our decision. An appeal is decided by a different person from the one who decided first. You can also turn to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body or to a court.

We aim for reliable operation but do not owe uninterrupted availability, for example during maintenance. We may change or discontinue features where your legitimate interests are preserved. We turn some features on step by step. We inform you of changes to these terms in good time in the app.
